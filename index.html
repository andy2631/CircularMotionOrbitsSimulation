Theory and model note

Velocity is tangent to a circular path. Centripetal acceleration and net centripetal force point towards the centre. Centripetal force is not an additional force: it is the net inward result of real forces such as tension, gravity or friction.

ac = v²/r   |   Fc = mac = mv²/r

Apparatus interpretation: the hanging mass is shown stationary. When speed is adjusted, this model treats its weight as the available tension and displays the new radius required by mhg = mrv²/r. A physical demonstration would require the hanging mass to be held at a reference mark while the rotating radius is established, then released only for the balance test.
